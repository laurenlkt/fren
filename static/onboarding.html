Welcome to Fren

What Fren is

Fren is a short, structured check-in for university students. It's an AI program, not a person. Each session helps you pick one thing to focus on, make a small plan, and think of a real person you could connect with.

Fren is not therapy, not medical advice and not crisis support. If you're struggling to stay safe, use the Get help now button at any time.

Fren is a research prototype.

Your privacy

  • No name, email or account. Fren gives this browser a random ID.
  • Fren keeps: your mood scores, session times, your small plan, and whether you chose to reach out to someone.
  • Your chat messages are deleted when each session ends.
  • If a safety check is triggered, Fren notes that it happened and the type of concern, but not your words.
  • You can delete all your data at any time in Settings.

Limits, by design

Fren is meant to be a small part of your week, not a big one. Each session lasts at most 10 minutes or 12 messages, and then it ends. There's also a gap before you can start the next one, which you choose here.
