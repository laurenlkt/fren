You deserve support from a real person right now

Fren is an AI program, not a person, and it can't help in a crisis. The people below can. They are free, confidential, and used to hearing from students who feel the way you might be feeling.

If you are in immediate danger

Call 999, or go to your nearest A&E.

Talk to someone now

  • Samaritans: call 116 123. Free, any time, day or night.
  • Shout: text SHOUT to 85258. Free, 24/7, if you'd rather text than talk.
  • NHS 111: call 111 for urgent advice when it isn't an emergency.

If you can, you could also tell someone you trust (a friend, family member, or someone at your university) how you're feeling.
